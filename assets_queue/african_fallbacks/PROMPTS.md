# African fallback art

- Religion/doctrine/building sheets: generated as clean 2x2 atlases from the
  supplied category prompts.
- Style references: actual installed EU5 religion, religious-aspect, and
  building DDS assets copied and converted under `vanilla_references/`; the
  three reference boards were passed directly to image generation.
- Constraints: dark navy EU5 icon field; archaeological material context;
  no text, glyphs, modern ethnicity, organized later religion, yellow wash, or
  shared crop across quadrants.
