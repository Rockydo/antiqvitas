# R28 rejected at AD 12 — Geopolitics presentation

R28 was a fresh, non-debug, 1920×1080 Rome campaign under the accepted smoke
fingerprint. It ran continuously from AD 1 to AD 12 without a crash. The AD 12
checkpoint had zero script errors, zero actionable warnings, no bankruptcies,
active construction across 31 payers, complete regional mercenary pools, and no
recurrence of the shared-captain hire race that rejected R27.

The run nevertheless failed the production gate. Active review of the always-
available Geopolitics panel exposed `Colonial Charters`, `Colonial Migration`,
`Cost of Colonies`, `Colonies`, `Recruit Explorer`, `Exploration Cost`, and
`Privateers`. These were stock localization surfaces around retained engine
mechanics, not unreachable technical identifiers. Because the same vocabulary
also appears in tooltips, alerts, automation, modifiers, the outliner, economy
rows, and messages, a panel-only wording patch would not be complete.

The campaign was stopped immediately. Evidence is retained in
`docs/screens/R28_FINAL_PRODUCTION_ROME_AD1_100/`, including the AD 12 panel
capture and checkpoint audit. R28 cannot count toward the final century gate;
the next candidate must restart from AD 1 after full validation and paired
smoke of the complete ancient expansion-presentation layer.
