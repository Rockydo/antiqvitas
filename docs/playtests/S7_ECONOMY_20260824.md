## What was still wrong after 2026-08-22

Static reachability treated “some tag can construct it” and
`opening_market_network` as enough. Live AD 1 still could not recover
ordinary army and Mediterranean food through buildings:

- Cheese dairies were unlocked on four culture profiles only (35 tags).
  Parthia, Suebi, Xiongnu, Judea, Armenia, and Nabataea could not build
  cavalry dairy. Rome had a seed, not a constructibility proof.
- Olive presses were unlocked on Iranian / Nile / Near-Eastern cards.
  **Rome could not construct an olive press.** World olive-oil supply was 0
  because remaining presses needed pottery and tools to staff.
- Grain mills accepted wheat only. Rice/millet/barley armies could not mill
  grain products from their own staple.
- Custom raw goods (papyrus, dates, sesame, barley, coconuts) had 0 live
  RGO supply. Cultivator seeds were generated after the previous playtest
  and had not been proven in a fresh start.
