## What was wrong

The previous close treated seeded workshops and a global “market network”
flag as proof that a player could obtain processed goods. The reachability
CSV had almost every processed good as `seeded` with `constructible_polities=0`.
Construction and civic upkeep still demanded steel, papyrus, incense, sailcloth,
iron hardware, and cured meat. Ship maintenance demanded dried fruit from
date-drying yards that are not an Italian industry. Live Rome showed
“Buildings Missing Inputs” and “Ships Are Not Repairing”.

Identity research had also stolen the only tools bloomery for Dacia, and
brickworks / pottery kilns / grain mills sat on Age IV–VI cards.
