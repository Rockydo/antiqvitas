# [PRO] Master-plan culture completion

The 23 culture categories previously absent or collapsed in the M4 atlas are now
ordinary rows in the canonical ledgers: `docs/m4/cultures.csv`,
`docs/culture_remap.csv`, `docs/m4/tag_profiles.csv`, and
`docs/m4/regional_profiles.csv`. No parallel culture catalogue or secondary
generator owns their output.

Twenty-two cultures have source-qualified AD 1 placements. Na-Dene remains a
definition-only culture because no controlled AD 1 location lies in its
source-appropriate northwestern range; assigning it elsewhere would fabricate a
population placement. Its symbol is retained through the canonical M12
non-historical initializer-presence ledger. Colchis, Osroene, and the Finland
regional profile use the new Colchian, Syriac, and Finnic definitions.

The canonical generators own all runtime definitions, colors, localization,
symbols, country definitions, compatibility presences, and start-pop output.
