# Cyprian epidemic art

Four AD 251-270 Roman-Mediterranean scenes were generated in one 2x2 atlas
against four actual EUV loading-painting references, then split locally. The
relief scene supplies the 1080x440 BC7 event surface; harbor disruption, grain
labor, and recovery crops remain retained derivatives. The art asserts no
specific pathogen, casualty count, uniform spread, or modern quarantine.

Sources: JRA-CYP-2015; JRA-CYP-2021.
